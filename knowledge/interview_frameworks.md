# STAR method for behavioural questions
Structure answers to "tell me about a time..." questions with STAR: Situation (set the context briefly), Task (your responsibility), Action (what you specifically did), Result (the measurable outcome). A strong answer spends most time on Action and Result, and quantifies the result.

# Tell me about yourself
Use a present-past-future arc: what you do now, the relevant experience that led here, and what you want next. Keep it under 90 seconds and tailor it to the role.

# Pitch structure
A clear pitch covers: the problem, who has it, your solution, why now, traction or proof, and a specific ask. Lead with the problem, not the product. End with one memorable line.

# Answering "why this company"
Show you researched them: reference a specific product, value, or recent news, then connect it to your own goals. Avoid generic praise that could apply to any company.

# Handling weaknesses
Name a real but non-fatal weakness, then describe the concrete steps you are taking to improve. Avoid clichés like "I'm a perfectionist".
