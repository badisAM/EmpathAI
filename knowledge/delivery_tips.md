# Filler words
Um, uh, like, you know, basically, and actually reduce perceived confidence. Replace them with a short silent pause. A few are fine; frequent fillers distract from your message.

# Speaking pace
Aim for roughly 120 to 160 words per minute. Too fast reads as nervous; too slow loses attention. Pauses after key points add emphasis and buy thinking time.

# Confidence and body language
Steady eye contact, an upright posture, and a calm facial expression signal confidence. Smiling at the start builds rapport. Fidgeting and looking away read as nervousness.

# Concision
Answer the question directly first, then support it. Rambling dilutes strong points. If you lose the thread, pause and restate your main point.
